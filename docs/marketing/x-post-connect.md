# X / Twitter — Connect post (intro + who I want to meet)

The #buildinpublic introduction genre: who I am, what I'm building, who I want
in my orbit. Goal is followers and inbound from developers, founders and
investors, plus surface area for the business-development co-founder search.
First person singular, no em-dashes, capability-led, no "we". **2–3 hashtags**,
end of post, per the standing kit in
[`x-post-build-apps.md`](x-post-build-apps.md). Links go in the **first reply**
(X downranks outbound links in the post body).

**Attach media natively:** the 60s promo cut or a dashboard screenshot showing
several apps over one graph. A connect post with a face also outperforms: a
photo or a screen-with-webcam still is worth considering.

## Notes

- **The raise wording is deliberate.** "Pre-seed conversations are starting"
  keeps it informational. Do not turn the post into a public "invest in my
  $250k round" ask: general solicitation of a securities offering on social
  media can force the round onto the accredited-verification path (Reg D
  506(c)). Interested investors will DM either way.
- The co-founder line is the real call to action; everything else builds the
  credibility for it. If one line has to go for length, cut the investor line
  before the co-founder line.
- Numbers stated (11 releases, ~8,500 stars, ~20 apps) match
  `../funding/founder-application-answers.md`.
  33 users / 570 visitors stays out of the post: strong as a conversion story
  in a pitch, weak as a social-proof number in public.
- Pin the post to the profile after posting; a connect post keeps recruiting
  from the pin long after the timeline moves on.
