Human voice, first person. 0.9.0 was "Work with the agent" but shipped with a bug
that broke the headline feature (the agent's propose tools never offered → it
fell back to plain chat); 0.9.1 is the fix. Two framings below — pick one.

## Option A — candid about the fix (recommended for the local-first crowd)

Brainstorm 0.9.1 is out — and honestly, it's the release 0.9.0 was supposed to be.

The whole point of this one: you and the AI work as a team. You ask it something,
and it drafts real things into your vault — a contact, a follow-up task, a note —
as little cards you approve or throw away. It proposes, you decide. It can't
actually save anything on its own, ever.

0.9.0 shipped a day ago with a dumb bug that broke exactly that — the agent quietly
fell back to plain chat and couldn't propose anything. 0.9.1 fixes it. If you
grabbed the broken one, it'll auto-update. Sorry about that.

Also in here:
– two machines on the same wifi sync a vault directly, no server in the middle
– the whole app now speaks French, German, Italian & Portuguese
– import your pages from Notion

Still 100% local, your keys, no cloud. getbrainstorm.online
